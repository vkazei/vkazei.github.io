About

I am a Geophysics Specialist – Advanced at ExxonMobil (since November 2026). Previously Geophysicist (Senior from 2023) at Aramco Americas Houston Research Center (November 2020–October 2026), working on machine learning and distributed acoustic sensing (DAS): DAS-based VSP imaging, deep learning-driven full-waveform inversion (FWI), and generative AI for subsurface modeling.

Associate Editor of Geophysics (2025–2027). Previously Research Scientist at KAUST in the Seismic Wave Analysis Group. PhD in Geophysics from Saint Petersburg State University.
